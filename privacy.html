Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Excel Guardianship Enterprise (XGEN) (Reg # B2015011402411, 12-13 Adonai Terrace, Pinto Road, Arima, Trinidad and Tobago) handles information accessed by our internal application Voices Uploader for managing our Facebook Page, Trinbago Reel Life.

1. Information We Access

Voices Uploader is an internal Page administration tool and does not offer public user logins. Via official Meta Graph API permissions, the application accesses only:

  • Page metadata and publishing access for the Trinbago Reel Life Facebook Page owned by us.
  • Public comments and the public display names of commenters who post greeting comments on our own Page videos.

2. How We Use Information

  • To publish daily automated video Reels to our Facebook Page.
  • To select and announce public viewer greeting shoutouts in our broadcasts and maintain an internal deduplication log so the same viewer is not selected repeatedly.

3. Data Storage, Sharing & Retention

  • All processing occurs locally on our private workstation.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or transfer Meta Platform Data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • Only minimal shoutout history records (commenter display name, comment ID, and date) are kept locally to prevent repeat shoutouts and are deleted upon request.

4. Contact & Data Deletion

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact Ulric Bain at mynosjunior@gmail.com or visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
