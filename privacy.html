Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Excel Guardianship Enterprise (XGEN) (Reg # B2015011402411, 12-13 Adonai Terrace, Pinto Road, Arima, Trinidad and Tobago) handles information accessed by our internal application Voices Uploader for managing our official Trinbago Reel Life channels on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok.

1. Information We Access

Voices Uploader is an internal administration tool and does not offer public user logins. Via official platform APIs (Meta Graph API, YouTube Data API, and TikTok Content Posting API), the application accesses only:

  • Authorized account identifiers, OAuth tokens, and content-publishing permissions for the official Trinbago Reel Life accounts owned by us.
  • Public comments and the public display names of commenters who post greeting comments on our own Page videos.

2. How We Use Information

  • To publish automated news summaries, breaking-news updates, and daily video Reels to our authorized Trinbago Reel Life accounts on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok.
  • To select and announce public viewer greeting shoutouts in our broadcasts and maintain an internal deduplication log so the same viewer is not selected repeatedly.

3. Data Storage, Sharing & Retention

  • All processing and OAuth tokens are stored locally on our private workstation.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or transfer platform data to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.
  • Only minimal shoutout history records (commenter display name, comment ID, and date) are kept locally to prevent repeat shoutouts and are deleted upon request.

4. Contact & Data Deletion

For privacy questions or removal requests, contact Ulric Bain at mynosjunior@gmail.com or visit our User Data Deletion Instructions page.
