User Data Deletion Instructions

Voices Uploader is operated by Excel Guardianship Enterprise (XGEN) exclusively for publishing and managing our Trinbago Reel Life Facebook Page. No external user accounts or login sessions are stored.

How to Request Deletion of Any Local Comment / Shoutout Record

  1. Send an email to mynosjunior@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request – Voices Uploader".
  2. Include your Facebook display name (or link to the comment on Trinbago Reel Life).
  3. We will permanently delete any matching entry from our local shoutout log and confirm completion within 48 hours.

Removing App Access via Facebook

If you previously authorized the app as a Page administrator, you can revoke access anytime via Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations, select Voices Uploader, and click Remove.
